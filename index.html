Get certified to write ACA business in 2027.

Two separate certifications, two separate portals, no overlap between them. FFM registration through CMS lets you write on HealthCare.gov. Georgia Access certification lets you write in Georgia, which runs its own marketplace. Both need to be done by October 1, ahead of open enrollment. Before you open either portal

Have these in front of you — both sites will time you out.

  • Your NPN. Your National Producer Number, exactly as it appears on nipr.com. Both portals key everything off it.
  • An active health insurance license in every state you intend to write. Georgia Access requires an active Georgia health license specifically.
  • Your legal name as licensed. If your CMS profile name doesn't match your license, your completion won't match up downstream. Middle initials count.
  • A personal email you'll keep and your phone for multi-factor codes. Don't use a shared agency inbox — these accounts are tied to you individually.
  • Two to three uninterrupted hours on a laptop. The training modules do not play reliably on phones, and progress can be lost on timeout.
Track 1

FFM Registration & MLMS Training

Runs entirely inside the CMS Enterprise Portal. You'll request access to an application, fill out a profile, take the training, sign two agreements, and print a certificate. Every step happens at portal.cms.gov.

  1. 1

    Create or sign in to your CMS Enterprise Portal account

    portal.cms.gov/myportal

    If you certified in a prior plan year, you already have an account — use it. Creating a second account is the single most common way agents lose a week.

    1. Existing account: sign in with your user ID and password, then complete multi-factor authentication.
    2. First time: choose New User Registration and select Agents/Brokers when asked for your user type.
    3. Enter your legal name, date of birth, home address and SSN exactly as they appear on your license. CMS verifies your identity against credit-bureau records (this is called RIDP) — a nickname or an old address will fail it.
    4. Set your user ID, password, security questions, and register a phone or email for MFA.
    Locked out
    Forgot your user ID or password, or failed identity proofing? Don't start over with a new account. Call the CMS Enterprise Portal help desk and have them unlock the one you have — see the help desk list at the bottom of this page.
  2. 2

    Request access to the training application

    My Portal → Add Application

    Your account by itself doesn't get you the training. You have to request a role on the FFM application — this is the step people skip.

    1. From My Portal, click + Add Application in the top right.
    2. Select the application: Federally Facilitated Marketplace (FFM)/Request for MLMS Training Access.
    3. Select the role: Agent Broker Training Access.
    4. For Reason for Request, type CMS Training. Anything short and factual is fine.
    5. Click Submit. You'll get a confirmation on screen and by email.
    Then what
    Approval is usually quick but is not always instant. You can check the status any time under My Access. Once it's approved, the tile in the next step appears on your portal home page.
  3. 3

    Open the Marketplace Training tile and launch MLMS

    My Portal → Marketplace Training – Agent Broker → MLMS Training

    Once your role is approved, a new tile shows up on your portal home page. Click the tile to expand it, then click the first link inside.

    1. On My Portal, find the Marketplace Training – Agent Broker tile and click it.
    2. A dark panel slides open under the tile with three links. Click the first one: MLMS Training.
    3. That hands you off to the Marketplace Learning Management System, which is a separate site (it runs on Cornerstone). Let it load fully.
    Bookmark
    The other two links are worth knowing. Registration Status Tracker is the searchable database you'll use in step 8 to confirm you're registered. Agent/Broker Helpdesk covers registration, training, Help On Demand and Find Local Help questions.
  4. 4

    Complete your MLMS profile and save it

    MLMS → Complete Agent Broker Training Profile

    The first time you land in MLMS each plan year it makes you confirm your profile. This is where your NPN gets attached to your training record, so slow down here.

    1. Check that your name matches your insurance license exactly. This is the field that has to line up for your completion to be credited.
    2. Enter your NPN and your business contact information.
    3. Race & Ethnicity is optional. CMS uses it to understand agent participation; answer or skip it, either is fine.
    4. Under Agency Affiliation, answer the Agency Owner question. Select No unless you are the owner, principal, or the one authorized representative registering on behalf of the agency. Only one person per agency should answer Yes.
    5. If you answered Yes, expand Business Entity Profile and fill it in. If you answered No, leave both Business Entity Profile and Web-Based Entity Profile collapsed.
    6. Scroll to the bottom and click Save. Nothing is stored until you do.
    Watch out
    If two people at the same agency both answer Yes to Agency Owner, the agency record gets tangled and takes a help desk ticket to unwind. Check with the office before answering Yes.
  5. 5

    Register for the right PY2027 curriculum

    MLMS home → Register from Training Options

    MLMS shows you several courses. Picking the wrong one means sitting through training that doesn't count for what you write.

    1. On the MLMS home page, use Select Training → Register from Training Options.
    2. Choose PY2027 AB Marketplace Training – Individual Marketplace. Take the Returning Agents and Brokers version if you completed FFM training for a prior plan year; take the New Agents and Brokers version if this is your first year. Returning is substantially shorter.
    3. Ignore Small Business Health Options (SHOP) unless you actually write group business. It's a separate, optional curriculum.
    4. A Spanish-language version of the same course is listed too — register for one or the other, not both.
    5. Once registered, the course drops into Your Transcript at the bottom of the page.
    Prior year
    Old plan-year courses stay on your transcript marked Inactive with a past due date. That's normal — they're history, not something to finish.
  6. 6

    Work through the curriculum and pass the exam

    Your Transcript → Open Curriculum

    This is the long part. Budget real time and do it on a laptop in one or two sittings.

    1. Click Open Curriculum next to the PY2027 course on your transcript.
    2. Complete each module in order. A module only counts when you reach its final screen — closing the window early loses that module's progress.
    3. Take the exam at the end. The required passing score is shown on the exam page; if you don't pass, you can retake it.
    4. Come back to the curriculum page and confirm every item shows as complete before you move on.
    Don't lose it
    MLMS times out on inactivity and the session does not always restore cleanly. Save your place by finishing the module you're in before you step away, rather than leaving it parked mid-module.
  7. 7

    Sign the agreements

    MLMS → Agreements

    Finishing the training does not register you. Signing the agreements does. Agents miss this every single year and then can't figure out why they don't show as registered.

    1. After the curriculum is complete, go to the Agreements section in MLMS.
    2. Read and electronically sign the Privacy and Security Agreement.
    3. Read and electronically sign the General Agreement for the Individual Marketplace.
    4. Confirm both show a signature date before you leave the page.
    This is the one
    Training complete + agreements signed = FFM registered. Training complete on its own = not registered, and your NPN won't appear on the completion list.
  8. 8

    Print your certificate and verify your status

    MLMS → View Completed Transcript · then the Registration Status Tracker

    Two pieces of proof: your own certificate, and CMS's public record that you're registered.

    1. From MLMS home, click View Completed Transcript and download your certificate as a PDF. Save it somewhere you'll find it again.
    2. Go back to the Marketplace Training – Agent Broker tile on My Portal and open the Registration Status Tracker.
    3. Search your NPN and confirm it shows you as registered for the current plan year.
    4. Send the certificate PDF and your NPN to the office so we can complete your carrier appointments.
    Lag time
    The tracker can take a few days to reflect a brand-new completion. If the certificate downloaded and both agreements are signed, you're done — just check the tracker again later in the week.
Track 2

Georgia Access Certification

Georgia left HealthCare.gov and runs its own state-based marketplace, so FFM registration does not cover Georgia business. This is a separate site with a separate login: certification.georgiaaccess.gov. It's shorter than the CMS track.

  1. 1

    Go to the Georgia Access certification site

    certification.georgiaaccess.gov

    Open it in a fresh tab. Your CMS credentials have nothing to do with this site — don't try to reuse them.

    1. Load the site and click Sign In in the top navigation.
  2. 2

    Create an account, or log in if you have one

    Sign In → Create Account

    If you certified with Georgia Access for a prior plan year, log in with that account. A duplicate account splits your training history.

    1. Returning: log in with the email and password you used last year. Use the password reset link rather than making a new account.
    2. New: choose Create an account and register with your work email.
    3. Verify your email address if prompted, then finish signing in.
  3. 3

    Fill in your agent profile

    Profile / agent information

    Same rule as the CMS side: your name and NPN have to match your license, or your certification won't tie back to you.

    1. Enter your NPN and your Georgia health insurance license number.
    2. Enter your legal name exactly as licensed, plus contact details.
    3. Note your agency affiliation if the site asks for it.
    4. Save the profile before moving on.
    Prerequisite
    You need an active Georgia health line of authority. If you're not licensed in Georgia yet, get the license through NIPR first — the certification won't do you any good without it.
  4. 4

    Launch the 2027 Agent Certification Training

    Training catalog / dashboard

    Once you're logged in, the available courses show as cards on your dashboard. Pick the one that matches your history.

    1. Find 2027 Returning Agent Certification Training if you were certified with Georgia Access for a prior plan year.
    2. If this is your first year with Georgia Access, take the new agent version instead — it's longer and covers the platform basics.
    3. Click the card to open the course and begin.
  5. 5

    Complete the modules and the assessment

    Inside the course

    Shorter than the CMS training, but the same rules apply — finish modules rather than abandoning them halfway.

    1. Work through each module in sequence; the course tracks completion per module.
    2. Take the assessment at the end and pass it.
    3. Sign any agent agreements or attestations the course presents. As on the CMS side, these are what actually make the certification count.
    4. Return to your dashboard and confirm the course reads as complete.
  6. 6

    Download your certificate and confirm you're certified

    Dashboard → certificate / status

    1. Download the certificate of completion as a PDF and save it with your FFM certificate.
    2. Check that your status on the dashboard reads as certified for PY2027, not just "training complete".
    3. Send the certificate to the office along with your NPN.
    Last step
    Certification is what lets you be listed and paid on Georgia Access business. Don't stop at the training completion screen.

When both tracks are done

Send the office two PDFs — your CMS/MLMS certificate and your Georgia Access certificate — along with your NPN. We'll confirm your NPN on the CMS completion list and get your carrier appointments moving. Keep both PDFs yourself too; carriers ask for them during appointment and again at audit.

When something goes wrong

Almost every problem on these two tracks is one of the following.

I finished the training but I'm not showing as registered

You almost certainly didn't sign the agreements. Go back into MLMS and check that both the Privacy and Security Agreement and the General Agreement show a signature date. Training completion alone does not register you with the FFM.

The Marketplace Training tile isn't on my portal home page

Your role request hasn't been approved, or was never submitted. Check My Access in the portal for a pending or missing request, and if there's nothing there, redo Track 1 step 2.

Identity proofing (RIDP) failed when I registered

This is a credit-bureau match, not a CMS decision. It usually fails on a name or address that doesn't match your credit file — a maiden name, a recent move, or a missing middle initial. Don't create a second account. Call the CMS Enterprise Portal help desk; they can walk you through manual verification.

Which training do I take — returning or new?

Returning if you completed that marketplace's training for a prior plan year, new if you didn't. The two systems are judged separately: you can be a returning agent on the CMS side and a new agent on Georgia Access, or the reverse. If you're unsure on the CMS side, look at your transcript — prior-year courses will be listed there.

Do I need both certifications?

It depends on where you write. FFM registration covers the states that use HealthCare.gov. Georgia runs its own state-based marketplace, so Georgia business requires Georgia Access certification. If you write in both, you need both. If you're only writing one, you still need an active health license in every state you touch.

A module froze, or I lost my progress

Close the course window, clear your browser cache, and reopen the curriculum from your transcript — completed modules should still be marked complete. Use a desktop browser, not a phone. If a module reopens at zero after you finished it, that's a help desk ticket, not something to redo blindly.

Can I get CE credit for this?

Not through MLMS. CMS states plainly that its training is free but that no CEUs are available through the MLMS. If you need continuing education hours, you have to take an approved vendor course separately.

Who to call

Match the problem to the desk — the wrong desk will just route you and cost you a day.
